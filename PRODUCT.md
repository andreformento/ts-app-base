# Product brief

Replace this file with your product. It answers WHAT to build; `CLAUDE.md` and
`docs/` answer HOW. An agent is told to read this before designing a feature,
so what you write here is what it will build.

Delete every heading you do not need, and delete this paragraph.

## What this is

One paragraph. What the product does, for whom, and what makes it different.

## Core concepts

The nouns that appear in the code. Name them here once, and keep those names in
`model/` — a concept renamed in conversation but not in code is how drift
starts.

- **Concept** — what it means, and what it is NOT.

## Settled decisions

Decisions already taken, so they are not relitigated. Include the reasoning, or
they will be.

- **Decision.** Why. What was rejected.

## Out of scope

What this product deliberately does not do, and what is merely deferred. The
distinction matters: deferred things shape the data model now.
